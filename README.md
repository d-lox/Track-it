# Track It 💸

A minimal, distraction-free personal finance and monthly money tracker designed to help you watch where every rupee goes and actually build savings—without overwhelming spreadsheets or intrusive ads.

Crafted with a signature **Liquid Glass** aesthetic, **Track It** blends fluid translucent surfaces and deep dark modes with hyper-practical daily tracking tools.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* **Liquid Glass Aesthetic:** Sleek, translucent frosted-glass cards, fluid animations, and a high-contrast dark UI that feels effortless to use every day.
* **Live Expense Meter:** Visualizes your monthly burn rate in real time so you know at a glance whether you are in the safe zone or approaching your threshold.
* **Smart Expense Limits:** Set custom monthly or category-wise spending caps and get clear visual feedback before you overspend.
* **Interactive Calendar View:** Track your financial habits day-by-day—spot high-spend weekends, zero-spend streaks, and recurring bills on a clean monthly grid.
* **Fast Entry & Breakdown:** Log expenses in seconds and get clean insights into where your money is going so you can cut waste and boost savings.
* **100% Local & Private:** Your financial data stays strictly on your device—no bank logins, no cloud snooping, and zero bloat.

---

## 🎯 Built For
Anyone who wants complete clarity over their monthly cash flow through a fast, minimalist interface that makes saving money feel intuitive rather than a chore.
